# World 4 Bro's - Credits & Asset Licensing Log

All assets, procedural shaders, audio synthesizers, and procedural canvas art generators in *World 4 Bro's* are 100% original, community CC0, or procedurally synthesized in-engine. No commercial, proprietary, or copyrighted assets are used.

## 1. Engine & Runtime Architecture
- **Rendering & Math**: Three.js (MIT License)
- **Noise Algorithms**: Simplex-Noise (MIT License)
- **Build Tooling**: Vite & TypeScript (MIT License)

## 2. Audio & Sound FX
- **Synthesized Audio**: In-engine Web Audio API procedural sound synthesizer (Original custom DSP algorithms for chops, pings, pops, rustles, honks, and ambient loops).
- **Procedural BGM**: CC0 / Original atmospheric wilderness and twilight ambient soundscapes.

## 3. Visual Assets & Shaders
- **Procedural 2.5D Textures**: Original canvas procedural ink-and-charcoal cutout texture generator (`TextureGenerator.ts`).
- **Normal Maps**: Procedural normal-vector bevel generators for 2.5D lighting.
- **Particle Systems**: Custom Three.js mesh & billboard particle manager.

## 4. Vehicle & Entity Design
- **Vehicles**: 100% original, fictional, slapstick vehicle models (`rusty_hatchback`, `clown_car`, `ice_cream_truck`, `golf_cart_deluxe`, `bathtub_buggy`, etc.). No commercial trademarks or real car brands.
- **Creatures & Zombies**: Original cartoon designs with green-puff cartoon defeat pops. Zero blood, zero gore.

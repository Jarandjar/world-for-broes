Effects and how they react

Acid, burn, explosion, stagger, electricity and more, as data. Visuals are the target look; numbers are starting values.

Twelve effects

Each one is a data row. Every effect also has an icon, a hand-lettered sound word and a plain-words tooltip, so colour is never the only signal.

Acid
From. Spitter zombies, acid pools, toxic rain, acid sprayers.
Does. Damage over time. Wears down metal gear. Leaves a lingering puddle.
Ends. Water, antidote gel, or about 6 s.
Burn
From. Fire, lava, flame weapons, explosions.
Does. Damage over time. Spreads to anything flammable. Lights the area.
Ends. Water, or about 5 s.
Explosion
From. Barrels, sappers, rockets, gas leaks.
Does. Area damage that fades with distance, knockback, a crater, big noise.
Ends. Instant.
Stagger
From. Heavy hits, blasts, knockback guns.
Does. A flinch, a stagger or a knockdown, depending on poise.
Ends. 0.2 to 1.5 s.
Electricity
From. Fences, tasers, storms, tesla coils.
Does. Shock damage and a short stun. Chains through wet and metal. Stalls vehicles.
Ends. Instant jolt, then the stun.
Cold
From. Tundra, ice weapons, frozen water.
Does. Slows, then freezes in place. Frozen things shatter when blasted.
Ends. Warmth or fire.
Spores
From. The Hollow, bogs, fungus.
Does. Drains sanity and makes vision swim.
Ends. Leaving the cloud, or light.
Goo
From. Slime zombies, sticky traps.
Does. Slows and sticks. Easy to hit.
Ends. Water, or struggling free.
Wet
From. Rain, water, squirt guns.
Does. Puts out fire, makes electricity chain, chills faster.
Ends. Dries over time.
Oil slick
From. Spills, wrecks.
Does. Slippery: you slide. Very flammable.
Ends. Burns off or is wiped.
Blind
From. Cream pies, smoke, salt glare.
Does. Blocks sight and ruins aim.
Ends. About 2 s.
Smoke
From. Fires, smoke pots.
Does. Hides you from sight. A little coughing damage.
Ends. Drifts away.

The stagger ladder

Hits should feel like something. Heavier things move you more.

Flinch
About 20% of poise
A 0.15 s slowdown. Aim is kept.
Stagger
About 50%
Cannot move for 0.5 s and steps back.
Stumble
About 80%
Spins and loses aim for about 1 s.
Knockdown
100% or a blast
Falls for 1.5 s and is easy to hit.

Every hit adds impact. Each creature has a poise value, and the share of poise a hit uses decides the step on the ladder. Size, armour and a riot shield add poise; the biggest zombies shrug off a flinch. Poise refills when not hit. All numbers are starting values.

Rules for all effects

Cartoon outcomes
Effects show as comic visuals: green drips, flames, a starburst. No gore. Burnt things turn to soot and ash.
Readable at a glance
Each effect has its own icon and shape, not just a colour. A small row of icons shows what is on you.
Kind to eyes
Flashes (electricity, explosions) follow a reduced-flashing setting alongside reduced motion. Nothing flashes fast by default.
Caps
A limit on active fires, puddles and chains, so a big fight cannot overload the game.
Same for players and zombies
The same rows work on bros, zombies, raiders, vehicles and structures. Immunities are data.
Server decides
In multiplayer the server applies and ends effects, so nobody can ignore a burn.

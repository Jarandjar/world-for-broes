What a biome is, as data

One definition holds
Name and moodTerrain mix and paletteEdge profilesGround decalsProps and set dressingHazards (tiles and objects)Weather and ambient effectsLight and sky colourZombie and animal spawn tableLoot flavourMusic and sound words
Why this works for many biomes
Terrain colours, the seam recipe, decals, props, hazards, weather and spawns are all data, and effects are data too. A new biome is a new definition plus a few drawn props, with no engine change. The same effect rows (acid, burn, electricity) are reused across biomes, so Toxic Marsh and Scrapyard share their rules and only differ in where and how often.
